Web app that lets users maintain a virtual bookshelf — tracking books they own and books they want.

**Why:** Personal project by a solo developer.
**How to apply:** All features should be framed around the user's book collection. Key identifiers are ISBN and ASIN. Keep scope focused on book ownership/wishlist tracking.
